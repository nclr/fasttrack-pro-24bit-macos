This installs Fast Track Pro 24-bit, a Core Audio plug-in that gives the M-Audio Fast Track Pro working 24-bit playback on macOS.

After installation, select Fast Track Pro 24-bit in System Settings → Sound → Output. The regular “FastTrack Pro” device disappears while the plug-in owns the card.

Sound stops for a few seconds at the end of the installation while Core Audio restarts.

Playback only: the card's inputs and MIDI are unavailable while the plug-in is installed. To remove it, open the Uninstall Fast Track Pro 24-bit package.
